Mission

Born from one heart, built for many dreamers.

The Dreamer Heart Fund began as a personal initiative to support artist Carlos Solis during his heart surgery, inspired by his artistic series “The Dreamer.” Today, it is a nonprofit effort to help artists and families in crisis feel less alone and more seen — turning art into anchor, and creativity into care.
