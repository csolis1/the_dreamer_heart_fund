Art Classes

Painting with Carlos Solis

A small, hands-on class led by surrealist artist Carlos Solis, founder of the fund's inspiration, “The Dreamer.” Carlos guides participants through building a painting from imagination outward — drawing on nature, memory, and personal symbolism — the same approach behind his own surrealist work. No prior painting experience is required.

Format
In-person, small group
Level
All levels welcome
Length
6:30 PM - 8:00 PM | 1.5 hours per session
Cost
$30 per session / $360 for 12 sessions
What to expect

Each session opens with a short talk from Carlos on the idea behind surrealist techniques, sketching and drawing warm-ups to loosen your hand. Classes will dive into playful Surrealist subconscious exercises and move into guided studio time. Sessions are held in small groups so Carlos can work with each participant individually.

Materials & what to bring

Every week, please bring your essential drawing gear: pencils, a sketchbook, and erasers. For designated painting days, make sure to pack your preferred paints, brushes, a palette, a canvas or acrylic paper, and a water cup.
